# Handover sheet

> [!NOTE]
> This file becomes public at the defense. Internal links (bender, Altium 365) are fine but won't work for outside readers.
> Don't put a personal phone number or address here.

## Purpose

We have all been rookies once, and were grateful to find clear instructions on how to get a project running and where it stands.
With this document you help whoever comes next and share with B105 what you have learned.

## How to use it

- **First week:** read the handover left by the previous rookie (`docs/handover.md` in their repository) and note anything missing.
  Spend the week getting everything they left running, unless your tutor says otherwise.
- **Every follow-up meeting with your tutor:** update this document.
- **When the report is finished:** complete it and ask another rookie to get the project running using only this document,
  as a review of a pull request; then add whatever was missing.
- **At the end of your thesis:** the final version stays here; also add it at the beginning of your report PDF.

The original template (in Spanish) and an example are in `bender/docs/Recursos/Handover`.

Short answers (2–3 sentences) and links are enough.

1. **Status.** What is the project and where are you leaving it? Is it a continuation of another project? (Link to its repository or to bender.)
2. **What works.** What works, what half-works and what doesn't? What is most likely to fail?
3. **Next steps.** What would you do next, and in what order? What would you change?
4. **Where everything is.** Links to the repositories (last working branch or tag), schematics/PCB (Altium 365 project, `hardware/` release) and data. Where the physical boards are, and how they are labelled.
5. **How to run it.** How do I get familiar with it? Steps to build, flash and run the main demo.
6. **Decisions.** What did you try and discard, and why?
7. **Lessons learned.** What did you find most useful? What advice would you give someone starting out?
8. **Contact.** Can you be contacted after you leave? How? (GitHub profile or professional email; give anything personal to your tutor privately.)
